Every treatment we offer, what it actually involves, and what it costs. Prices below are sample starting points — your quote comes after we have looked properly.

General Check-up & Scaling

A proper look at every tooth, your gums and your bite, followed by a scaling and polish to clear the hardened plaque a toothbrush cannot reach.

What to expect

  • A full examination and, where needed, digital X-rays
  • Ultrasonic scaling and a polish, usually in the same visit
  • A written note of anything we want to keep an eye on

Teeth Whitening

In-clinic whitening that lifts years of coffee, tea and teh tarik staining in a single appointment, with a take-home kit to keep the result.

What to expect

  • A shade check and a gum health review before we start
  • One 60 to 90 minute session in the chair
  • Custom trays and gel so you can top up at home

Root Canal Treatment

When decay reaches the nerve, a root canal saves the tooth instead of removing it. Done properly, it should feel no worse than a large filling.

What to expect

  • Local anaesthetic throughout, at your pace
  • Usually one or two visits depending on the tooth
  • A crown recommendation if the tooth needs protecting

From RM450

Gum Disease Treatment

Bleeding when you brush is not normal. We treat gum disease from the early stages through to deep cleaning below the gum line.

What to expect

  • Pocket depth charting so we can measure progress
  • Deep cleaning under local anaesthetic where needed
  • A home care routine built around your actual habits

From RM150

Dental Implants

A titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth, topped with a crown matched to the teeth beside it. The closest thing to the real thing.

What to expect

  • A CT scan and a written plan before anything begins
  • Placement under local anaesthetic, healing over a few months
  • A crown colour-matched to your natural teeth
