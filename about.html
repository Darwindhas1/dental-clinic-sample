Our Story

A Clinic Built Around Comfort

We opened in Ipoh with one idea: dentistry should not be something you dread. Everything here — the pace, the pricing, the room itself — is arranged around that.

How We Started

Ten Years of Familiar Faces

Dr. Aisyah Rahman opened a two-chair clinic off Jalan Sultan Idris Shah in 2016, after a decade in a busy government practice where nobody had time to explain anything. She wanted the opposite: longer appointments, plain language, and prices quoted before treatment rather than after.

Ten years later we have three dentists, a full hygiene team, and a lot of patients who first came in for an emergency filling and never left. We still run the clinic the same way — unhurried, honest, and happy to answer the question you feel silly asking.

We treat whole families, from a toddler's first check-up to grandparents' dentures, and we are proud that most of our new patients arrive because somebody they know sent them.

What We Stand For
